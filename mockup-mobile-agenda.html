Mobile Agenda View Mockups

Two options for replacing the calendar grid on mobile (≤ 768px). Both use the same Abyss design language. Tap/scroll to imagine interaction.

Option A — Scrolling Agenda
A continuous scrollable list of days with events underneath. Days without events are collapsed to a single line. Today is highlighted. Sticky day headers keep you oriented while scrolling. This is the pattern Google Calendar and Apple Calendar use on mobile.

Option B — Date Ribbon + Day List
A horizontally scrollable ribbon of dates at the top, with colored dots indicating events. Tapping a date shows that day's events below in a card list. Gives a quick visual overview of the week while focusing on one day at a time. Similar to Outlook mobile.
